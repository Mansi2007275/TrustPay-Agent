# TrustPay Agent — Pitch

"We don't give AI unlimited access to money. We give it limited authority — and a conscience."

TrustPay is an autonomous financial agent with bounded authority. It executes
routine payments on its own, but continuously evaluates risk, business policy,
vendor reputation, and behavioral anomalies. When a decision exceeds its
authority, it stops and asks a human — with evidence. Every action is auditable.

See TrustPay_Agent_Full_Spec.md for the complete feature list and architecture.
